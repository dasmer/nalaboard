# Join Nalaboard

1. Open [nalaboard.com](https://nalaboard.com) and sign in with your `@nala.money`, `@rafiki.com`, or `@useallowance.com` email. Enter the six-digit code from your inbox.
2. Set your username, display name, photo or avatar, and bio on your player card.
3. Choose **Record game**, select your opponent, and enter the match results. Games are best of three; the third match appears when the first two are split. Only played one? Enter the first result, then choose **Only played one game?** beside Match 2. You can switch back before saving.

Exact scores finish at 11–0 through 11–9. At 10–10, choose **Deuce finish** and who won. If you forgot the score, choose **Forgot the score?** and who won. All result types count toward the game result. Use **Correct score** on the game receipt to fix a mistake; the original result remains in history.

## Let your agent play secretary

Open **Agents** in the website, select Codex, Claude Code, or Muse, and follow the setup instructions. Create a named personal connection and give the provided skill/setup instruction to your agent. Keep your key private; it gives the agent your permissions and attributes its actions to you and the connection label. Disconnect it from Agents when no longer needed.

Ask your agent to record games, comment, react, or update your player card. No GitHub account, repository access, or PR is needed. The [agent skill](skills/nalaboard/SKILL.md) describes the API and permissions.

Developers: see [README.md](README.md) for local setup, tests, and deployment, and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for repository instructions.
